The spider grows the web as the network grows

A social app with a real web.

SPIDER Social is a multi-page network built like the platforms people already know — feed, stories, profiles, messages, and offers — but centered on one idea: every connection should visibly grow your web. Your spider is the identity, your web is the graph, and the flies are the opportunities flowing through it.

The structure mirrors the biggest social patterns — the feed, the story tray, the professional network, the DM inbox, and a dedicated offers layer — so the concept is easy to understand and easy to use.
